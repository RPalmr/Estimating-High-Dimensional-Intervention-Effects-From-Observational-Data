Estimating High-Dimensional Intervention Effects From Observational Data
======================================================================

Implementation of Maathuis, Kalisch and Bühlmann (2009), arXiv:0810.4214v3, DOI:10.1214/09-AOS685. The supplied paper is in `paper/`.

The scope is Gaussian single-variable total intervention effects: original PC with Fisher Z tests, global DAG enumeration (Algorithm 1), parent-set multiplicities (Algorithm 2), and local IDA (Algorithm 3). Experiments follow Section 6 and include one additional seeded random Gaussian DAG dataset. No latent-confounder or joint-intervention extensions are included.

Implementation and validation are in progress. Experiment results will identify their exact settings, seeds, data provenance, and any departures from the published protocol. Generated results are never represented as the paper's original numerical outputs.
